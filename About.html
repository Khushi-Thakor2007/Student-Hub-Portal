About Student Hub

Student Hub is a platform that helps students access academic resources, manage events, communicate with faculty, and stay updated with campus activities through one easy-to-use portal.


Mission

To provide students with a centralized platform for learning, communication, and campus engagement.

Vision

To create a smarter, connected, and collaborative student community.


Features
  • Student Dashboard
  • Profile Management
  • Event Updates
  • Announcements
  • Feedback System
  • Admin Management
