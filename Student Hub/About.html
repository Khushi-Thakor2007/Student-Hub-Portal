About Student Hub

Student Hub is a centralized platform designed to help students access academic resources, manage events, communicate with faculty, and stay updated with campus activities through one easy-to-use portal. It provides a simple and organized environment where students can easily manage their academic and campus activities.

🎯 Mission

To provide students with a centralized platform for learning, communication, academic management, and campus engagement while making information easily accessible.

🌟 Vision

To create a smarter, connected, and collaborative student community by using modern technology to simplify campus life.

✨ Features

  • 📊 Student Dashboard
  • 👤 Profile Management
  • 📚 Study Materials
  • 📅 Academic Calendar
  • 🎉 Event Updates
  • 📢 Notifications
  • 📝 Feedback System
  • ⚙️ Admin Management
